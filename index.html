Getting Around

Public buses in Taniti City

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Private buses serve other parts of the island.

Other options

  • Taxis are available in Taniti City.
  • Rental cars are available from an agency near the airport.
  • Bicycles and helmets are available to rent; helmets are required by law.
  • Taniti City and Merriton Landing are walkable areas.

Visitor Information

CurrencyTaniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and many accept major credit cards.
PowerElectrical outlets are 120 volts, as in the United States.
LanguageMany younger Tanitians speak fluent English; less English is spoken in rural areas.
Healthcare and SafetyTaniti has one hospital and several clinics. Violent crime is rare, though petty theft occurs.

Some attractions and restaurants close on national holidays, so plan ahead.
